Compass Announces University Partnership Initiative to Support International Students

Compass Newsroom   /   Partnership initiative

NEW YORK: Compass, a banking guidance platform designed for international students, announces a new partnership initiative with university international student offices to help students navigate the U.S. banking system.

Through this initiative, Compass will provide universities with ready-made banking resources that can be integrated into orientation programs and student onboarding materials. International students will receive personalized banking recommendations, clear information about account requirements, and guidance on preparing the necessary documents before visiting a bank.

The partnership will also help universities reduce repetitive banking questions for student advisors and better understand common financial challenges through anonymized student insights. Universities will have the option to access a co-branded version of Compass through an annual licensing model.

By combining university support with accessible banking guidance, Compass aims to make the transition to life in the United States easier and help international students make more confident financial decisions.
